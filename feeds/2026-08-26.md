**🕒 06:33 👤 @DrewNaylor@mastodon.online**
💬 Just figured out how to set the default tab width in KDevelop for Go to 4 since 8 was bothering me and I kept having to change it.

You will need to go to:
  - "Settings"
  - "Configure KDevelop"
  - "Editor"
  - "Open/Save"
  - "Modes & Filetypes"
  - "Filetype: Sources/Go"
  - in the "Variables" textbox, paste this; modify for your needs:
  - `kate: tab-width 4; indent-width 4; replace-tabs off; replace-tabs-save off;`
