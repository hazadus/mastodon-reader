💬 my experience with golang is there are some things it is amazingly good at and some things it is amazingly bad at. so if you find yourself spending more than an hour and not making any headway, its time to give up on go and use another language. otherwise you will spend the whole day and still make no headway, give up on go and use another language.

people (including me) pile on php and the defenders say 'but those ppl are working on it in their spare time' but what if a language had an alphabet megacorp behind it and it still had vast swathes of feces smeared across the codebase?

i remember when go first came along, they promised they were never going to write go in go, so it would always be easy to bootstrap no matter what system you used. then one day i suppose there was a change in management and they suddenly said 'go is mature, we are gonna rewrite go in go, if you don't like it you can suck my'

personally i think this sudden change was to the detriment of golang. now if some part of the codebase is crud, better programmers will just go down a lower level and use that ('net' instead of 'http' etc). but that still leaves the crud on top. if the lower layer was in c, it would have incentivised ppl to fix up the upper layers.

maybe as an amateur my standards are too high. a professional would have internalised 'thats just how it is' by now 🙂

As far as I can tell, none of this has to do with Immich per se, it's a change to an upstream runtime. To the best of my knowledge, none of the telemetry goes to immich.

- Adding `ORT_DISABLE_TELEMETRY=1` to your `.env` file, and/or
- Using pi-hole or another ad-blocking DNS server to intercept and block the requests.

PLEASE DO NOT PILE ON THE IMMICH TEAM. This looks like a tiresome update to a dependency and not any intentional action on Immich's behalf.
