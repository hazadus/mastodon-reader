I strongly encourage you to use anything but a language that can be compiled by different totally independent compilers/interpreters.

My favourite are [#Oberon](https://snac.tesio.it?t=oberon) and [#C](https://snac.tesio.it?t=c).

[#Myrddin](https://snac.tesio.it?t=myrddin) and [#Hare](https://snac.tesio.it?t=hare) are potential candidates (despite having one single implementation) because of their current simplicity.

Any language complex enough that it can only be compiled by one or two corporate-backed compilers are going to backfire on your users in the long (or even short) run.

[#Rust](https://snac.tesio.it?t=rust) is way too complex.

So is [#Python](https://snac.tesio.it?t=python). And [#Zig](https://snac.tesio.it?t=zig) (that still requires [#LLVM](https://snac.tesio.it?t=llvm)).

[#Go](https://snac.tesio.it?t=go) is from [#Google](https://snac.tesio.it?t=google), so I avoid anything written in it.
